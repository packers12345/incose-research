METHOD
  A `modify` is neither an accept nor a reject, but scoring the reviewer as a
  binary classifier needs one. All 19 modifies were labelled by hand against
  the criterion the AI invoked, the AI's suggested text, the SME's final text
  and the SME's note. The test: does the edit accept the premise of the flag
  as categorised? Adopting the AI's words for a different reason than the AI
  gave is not an endorsement.

WHAT 'AUTO-CHECK' MEANS
  Every hand label in the tables below is shown next to the answer a simple
  automatic rule would have given, purely as a sanity check on the hand
  labelling. The automatic rule is: look at the words the AI ADDED to the
  requirement, then see how many of them the reviewer kept in their final
  text. Kept most of them -> guess ENDORSE. Dropped most -> guess REJECT. It
  counts words, so it cannot tell why a reviewer kept a word, and it is fooled
  when two people reach the same wording for different reasons, or when a
  reviewer rewrites the same fix in their own words. It is NOT used in any
  published number - the hand labels are. It agrees with them on 9 of 19; the
  disagreements are listed so you can see where word-counting and reading
  diverge.

TALLY
  19 modifies: 12 endorse, 7 reject. The auto-check agrees on 9 of 19. SME 2 =
  2, SME 3 = 4, SME 5 = 3, SME 6 = 10; the remaining reviewers logged none.

  12 of the 19 sit on cells the ground truth calls clean, so the policy mostly
  moves items between FP and TN, shifting TNR/FPR more than TPR/FNR. The 7 on
  GT-violation cells move TP against FN and so bear directly on TPR.

  PROVENANCE CAVEAT. The nine decisions for SME 2, SME 3 and SME 5 were
  adjudicated without reference to ground truth. The ten for SME 6 were
  adjudicated with each item's GT status visible, and came out perfectly
  aligned with it - all five GT-violation modifies endorsed, all five GT-clean
  modifies rejected. That is also the pattern anchoring would produce. The
  earlier nine show no such alignment: five of their seven GT-clean modifies
  were endorsed, producing false positives. SME 6's TNR and TPR should be
  treated as provisional until the block is re-labelled blind.

  marked in the ADJUDICATED comment block in reviewer_rates.py were written
  when the labels were set; the other 12 were added later to explain the
